# Membership pitch

Written 2026-10-26 by the senior salesperson role. Price placeholders `{PRICE}` and `{PRICE_ANNUAL}` are the market analyst's to fill. Placement is the UX expert's call; section 5 says where I'd put it and the one place it must never go.

The rule this whole document obeys: we sell only what ships on launch day. A promise is listed as a promise, never as a feature.

## 1. The offer and its name

### Three names, one pick

| Name | Why it works | Why not |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Hygienics Membership** | Plain, honest, uses the owner's own word ("members"). "Join" is the verb, "member" is the noun, nothing to decode. | Not clever. |
| Clear Path Plus | Ties to the product people already used for free. | Reads like a software tier, and the membership is more than the Path. |
| The Fold | Nods to the brand mark (Continuous Fold); "inside the fold" is a warm image. | Cryptic on a pricing page; needs explaining, which a calm brand shouldn't spend words on. |

**Pick: Data Hygienics Membership.** The call to action is "Become a member" and the short form on buttons is "Join". A calm brand sells with plain names.

### What a member gets, sorted by whether it exists

**Ships today (sell these):**
- **Ask Data Hygienics.** A chat that answers only from our published research, cites the page it drew from, and says "we don't know yet" when it doesn't know. Fair-use caps (40 questions a day, a monthly token budget) keep it sustainable; nobody will hit them in normal use. Built at `/tools/ask`; needs the usage table migration before launch.
- **Your Path on every device.** Save your home and business paths to your account and pick them up anywhere. Exists today through the magic-link save; membership makes the account the member account.
- **Home and business under one membership.** One account runs both paths. A sole proprietor doesn't pay twice for being a person and a business.
- **Independence, funded by you.** We sell no security products, take no referral fees, run no ads or sponsorships. The membership is the money that keeps that true. This is a feature, and the one members will repeat to friends.
- **Yours to leave.** Cancel any time in two clicks, export or delete your path, and your questions to Ask are never stored by us.

**Promised, not sold until shipped (do not put these in the pitch yet):**
- The monthly note, "What changed, and whether your path changed." Roadmap milestone three. The pitch below works without it; when it ships, it becomes proof point two.
- Printable cards at `/cards` (safe-word card, card by the phone, the one-page AI rule). Milestone two. Verdict pages already print as a half-page card, which is free and stays free.
- Early verdicts (members see a new Threat Check verdict before it's public). Cheap to build; not built.

**Free and staying free (say so in the pitch, it's part of the trust):** the Path itself, every guide and threat page, the Threat Check and its verdicts, the step pages, the glossary.

## 2. One membership, two doors

**One membership, one price, two doors.** A member chooses Home or Business when they arrive, and can run both. The reasons: the audience overlaps (the person who got handed IT at a ten-person firm is also someone's adult child), the costs are the same (the assistant and the account don't care which path you run), and a single price is the only price a calm pricing page can carry without a comparison table. Two products would force a "which am I" decision before the value is clear, and would invite the question "why is business more expensive for the same chat."

**The Documentation Pack stays a one-time business product** at its current $299: the full Cyber Audit, the nine policies, the incident plan, the remediation plan. It is a deliverable, not a relationship, and businesses buy deliverables when a client, lender or insurer asks for paper. Relationship to membership: members get the Pack at a member price (analyst to set; a round number like a third off) and the Pack's buyers get a free month of membership so they meet Ask. The Pack is where business revenue comes from; membership is where retention comes from.

**Ongoing Protection** ($49 a month) is retired as a public offer. Its promised recurring value is exactly what membership now delivers, at a price a household can also pay. Existing subscribers are grandfathered as members with the Pack's entitlements, and the entitlement code already treats them as paid.

## 4. Objections and answers

**"Why pay when the Path is free?"**
The Path is free and stays free. Membership is for the moment the Path can't anticipate: the text you're holding, the setting you can't find, the call that didn't feel right. Ask answers that, from our research, in a minute. And the money is what keeps the free part free of ads and affiliate deals.

**"I already have Aura or LifeLock."**
Keep it if it's working for you. Those products monitor and insure; they tell you after something happened. We're the other half: the habits that stop it, in order, and a place to ask before you act. We also sell no product, so our answer to "do I need this" is allowed to be no.

**"I don't trust an AI chat with my security."**
Neither would we, which is why Ask only answers from pages we wrote and sourced, shows you the page, and says "we don't know yet" instead of guessing. It never asks for passwords, codes or account numbers, and your questions aren't stored. You can read every rule it follows.

**"I'm not technical."**
Good. Every step is written four ways, including "just tell me what to do." Ask is for the person who was handed a phone, a router or a company inbox without a manual. If a word trips you up, ask what it means.

**"My business is tiny."**
That's who we built this for: the owner, the office manager, the person who got handed IT. One membership covers your business path and your home path. If a client or lender asks for paperwork, the Documentation Pack is there, at a member price.

## 5. What "central" means without breaking the calm

**Where joining appears, once per page, never twice:**
- **Navbar:** "Join" as a quiet text link beside "Sign in" (signed-out visitors only). The primary button stays "Map your path": the free thing stays the loudest thing.
- **Homepage:** the membership band after "How the Path works" and before the three doors. One band, one button.
- **Path reveal:** the save-or-join moment described above, after the path is shown and the first step is visible. This is the most natural place on the site, because the visitor has just received something worth keeping.
- **Pricing page:** membership first, Documentation Pack second, legacy plans not shown.
- **Ask's non-member state:** the page itself is the pitch. Someone who followed a link to Ask without membership sees what it does and the price. No redirect.
- **Hubs and the business tools catalog:** one line at the bottom ("Members get Ask"), not a card.

**Never:**
- Inside a Threat Check verdict, above or below the fold, while someone may be in the middle of an incident. The verdict ends with a Path chip and nothing else.
- Inside a step's "Do it" list, in the first-step card, or in the quiz.
- As a popup, a slide-in, an exit prompt, a countdown, a "limited time" line, or a dismissible bar. Those are the enemy's tools.
- On the privacy, terms and contact pages.

**How often:** a visitor should meet the membership once per visit on average, and twice only if they go looking (pricing page). No remarketing copy, no urgency vocabulary, no social proof we can't source.

**Tone rules for every variant:** second person, no fear words, no numbers without a named source (none appear above), "AI" never "SI", and the free offer is named in the same breath as the paid one.

## 6. Before the pitch goes live

A checklist, in order:
1. **Ask works for members in production:** the `assistant_usage` migration applied, the route returning answers for a paying account, the usage meter counting.
2. **The member plan exists end to end:** a Stripe product and two prices (monthly, annual) in the mode the site runs in; `lib/stripe/prices.js` filled in; the webhook mapping the prices to a `member` plan; `getPlanAccess` granting `canAccessAssistant` and the Path sync to `member`; the pricing page reading the real price IDs so checkout stops failing closed.
3. **The save-or-join moment on the Path reveal** built, with the free save first.
4. **Privacy page updated:** one plain sentence that questions to Ask are sent to Anthropic's API to generate the answer and are not stored by us, with a link to the assistant's rules.
5. **Cancellation in two clicks** from the dashboard through the Stripe billing portal, tested once.
6. **Export and delete** for the saved path, tested once, and the "yours to leave" line pointing at where to do it.
7. **Ongoing Protection removed from the pricing page**, grandfathered subscribers unaffected (verified against `access.js`).
8. **Member price on the Documentation Pack** wired, or that line removed from the pricing card until it is.
9. **The monthly note stays out of the copy** until the first one has been sent.
10. **Analytics event:** `path_saved` already fires; add nothing else. The membership's own conversion shows up in Stripe.

If any of 1 through 7 is not true on launch day, the pitch waits. The brand's whole position is that it doesn't sell what it can't deliver.
