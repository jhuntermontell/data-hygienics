# Data Hygienics brand kit

The master identity is the **Continuous Fold**: a three-color standalone mark paired with a custom outlined wordmark based on Avenir Next Demi Bold. Its upper-right **signature clean cut** is a defining feature—it makes the silhouette proprietary and represents removing noise and ambiguity. Inter remains the product and website typeface.

## Raster exports

Transparent PNG exports accompany the SVG masters, including 1024, 512, 192, and 32 pixel mark sizes. Purpose-drawn `mark-micro-24.png` and `mark-micro-16.png` exports are included for favicons and compact UI. Use the full-color master at 40 px and above; use the micro marks at 16–24 px. SVG should remain the source for production, print, embroidery digitization, and future exports.

## Production note

Use the full-color mark on light backgrounds. On navy caps and other dark merchandise, use the one-color white mark; do not attempt the three-color mark in embroidery at small sizes. Preserve the clean cut and central opening in every reproduction.

Before public launch or filing, complete a professional trademark and visual-similarity review and confirm commercial rights for the outlined wordmark artwork. The cap image in the guide is a merchandise visualization; embroidery vendors should digitize from the supplied SVG master rather than the mockup.
